How it works

  1. Comment an animal on any of our Reels or Facebook posts. Your Instagram name, or your first name and last initial on Facebook, becomes its name.
  2. Your creature is designed and added to the ocean. Most arrive within a day. New animals take a little longer.
  3. Find it here: you're now a resident of the ocean. Watch for it in future Reels.

We reached 5,000 residents, so the whole ocean is glowing until 21 November.

Hide my name

Your name shows on your creature. To hide it, comment "hide my name" on any of our Reels or posts and it will say Name hidden instead. You can still find your creature by typing your exact name. To bring it back, comment "show my name". Your name comes off this site within a few hours, and we won't name you in new videos. You can also message us "hide my name" or "show my name" on Instagram (@the_ocean_of_us) or Facebook, or email theoceanofus@gmail.com.

Age

The Ocean of Us is for ages 13 and up, the same as Instagram and Facebook. Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before you join or buy anything. If a parent or guardian tells us a creature belongs to a child under 13, we take it and the name off this site and leave them out of new videos.

The creatures and this ocean are drawn by computer code, built with help from AI tools. The voice in our Reels is AI-generated. Extinct and mythical creatures are labelled as reconstructions or legends.

The Ocean of Us is made by one person in Victoria, Australia.
