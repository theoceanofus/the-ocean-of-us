Shop terms

What you get

A digital item for one creature in The Ocean of Us. It shows on the site, on the creature's card, and in our Reels when that creature is already on screen. It's just for fun and can't be swapped for money.

Price

A$3 each, paid through Stripe. No GST.

Giving

10% of the price of every item sold goes to an ocean conservation charity, paid each month. Refunded items and giveaway prizes don't count.

Delivery

Within about three hours. If it hasn't appeared after a day, email us.

The Spotlight

For 7 days from when it appears, the creature glows gold in the ocean and on its card, and its name is listed at the top of the ocean and first on the Everyone page. Buying it again while it shows adds 7 more days. A hidden name isn't listed, but the creature still glows. It doesn't put a creature in a Reel.

Packs

A pack is three items for A$6. Each goes on as if bought on its own: it replaces the old one of its kind, and a Spotlight lasts 7 days. A refund takes all three off.

Christmas bundle

The Christmas bundle is five items for A$10: a Santa hat, sparkles, a little fish, a starlight trail and the Spotlight for 7 days. It's sold until 25 December 2026 (Melbourne time). Each goes on as if bought on its own: it replaces the old one of its kind, and the Spotlight lasts 7 days. A refund takes all five off.

Refunds

For any reason within 14 days of buying. Email theoceanofus@gmail.com with the date and the creature's Instagram username or Facebook name. When we refund it, the item comes off. You also have rights under the Australian Consumer Law that these terms don't take away.

Gifts

Anyone can buy an item for any creature. The card says "A gift" and never names who bought it.

Taking extras off

The creature's owner can comment "remove my extras" on any of our Reels. That doesn't refund the buyer, who can still ask within 14 days.

If someone leaves

Their extras go with their creature. A refund isn't automatic, but the 14-day rule still applies.

Your collection

Everything bought for a creature stays in its collection, even after a newer one of the same kind goes on. To switch, the creature's owner comments “wear” and its name on any of our Reels, like “wear crown”. A refunded item leaves the collection. The Spotlight isn't kept: it lasts its 7 days.

Hats and Christmas items

A creature wears one hat at a time: if it has a bought hat and a Christmas hat, the newer one shows, or the one last asked for with “wear”. Christmas items show from 1 November to 6 January, and come back each November.

Under 18

Ask a parent or guardian before you buy.
