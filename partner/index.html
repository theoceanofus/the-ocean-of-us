Partner with us

The Ocean of Us is a live animated ocean where every creature belongs to a real person. Comment an animal on our Reels and it's drawn and added, named after you. Your brand can support one of its zones.

Who's here

  • 5,324 creatures, each one a real person on Instagram or Facebook
  • 506 kinds of animal, designed for the people who asked
  • About 900 visits a day to the ocean (Cloudflare Web Analytics, 12 November 2026), mostly from the United States, India, South Africa, Malaysia and the United Kingdom, nearly all on phones
  • Our most-seen Reel (6 November 2026) reached 25,833 Instagram accounts in its first week. Of our 5 Reels from 10 to 12 November 2026, the middle one reached 2,768 accounts in its first day or two (Instagram's own numbers, read 14 November 2026)

Creatures and kinds of animal as of 15 November 2026.

What a zone sponsor gets

  • Your name on one zone (The Shore, The Reef, The Kelp Forest, Open Water, The Deep and The Abyss of Legends): "Paid for by" your brand where visitors swim into it, and "This zone is paid for by" your brand on the Everyone page, so young visitors can tell it's a paid partnership. A charity shows as "Partner charity".
  • A mention in one of our Reels that month, posted with Instagram's Paid Partnership label.
  • Your name and link on this page while you're a partner.
  • A one-page report each month: the residents living in your zone and the Reels it was in. Made from daily totals only, never anything about who someone is.

Your first month is free. After that, A$150 a zone, a month. One brand per zone at a time. Ocean and wildlife charities are free.

For small businesses

If your business has fewer than 20 staff, you can support a zone for A$75 a month. Or become an Ocean friend for A$40 a month, with no zone needed: your name and link on this page, and a thank-you in one of our Reel captions that month, posted with the Paid Partnership label. The first month is free either way.

Who we work with

Many of our residents are young, so we only partner with brands that are right for them. We don't take:

  • food or drink high in fat, sugar or salt
  • alcohol, vaping or tobacco
  • gambling, betting, loot boxes or crypto
  • dating, weapons or anything for adults only
  • anything that collects children’s personal details

Sponsorship never changes anyone's creature, and we never share our residents' details.
