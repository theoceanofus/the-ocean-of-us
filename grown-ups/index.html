For grown-ups

A short guide for parents, guardians and teachers.

What it is

The Ocean of Us is a free, live animated ocean. Someone comments an ocean animal on one of our Instagram Reels or Facebook posts, and we draw that animal and add it to the ocean, named after them. It's made by one person in Victoria, Australia, with help from AI tools.

Age

It's for ages 13 and up, the same as Instagram and Facebook. Under 18s should ask a parent or guardian before joining or buying anything. If you tell us a creature belongs to a child under 13, we take it and the name off this site and leave them out of new videos.

What shows publicly

Only the creature and the Instagram username, or for Facebook a first name and last initial. Comments and messages aren't shown on this site. There are no accounts, no chat between visitors and no ads.

What we count

To see what helps people buy and share, and how much each zone of the ocean is visited, the site counts how many times a few things happen each day, like the shop opening, Pay being tapped or a zone being visited. Only daily totals are kept, for about 13 months, never who did what: no cookies, no IP addresses, no device details and no accounts. Totals under 5 are never shown. The full list is on the privacy page.

Hiding a name or removing a creature

Comment "hide my name" or "remove my creature" on any of our Reels, or email us. A parent or guardian can ask too. A name comes off this site within a few hours, and we won't name that person in new videos. You can also message us "hide my name" or "show my name" on Instagram (@the_ocean_of_us) or Facebook, or email theoceanofus@gmail.com.

The shop

Optional extras for a creature, like a hat or a little friend, cost A$3 each. Nothing is random, there are no subscriptions, and nothing is needed to take part. Stripe takes the payment, and we never see card details. Refunds are given for any reason within 14 days. "Ask someone to buy it" only sends a link: nothing is paid until a grown-up chooses to.

Giving one as a present

You can give an extra to a young person's creature as a present, for Christmas or any time. In the shop, type their Instagram username, or the first name and last initial they use on Facebook, pick something (the packs are three things for the price of two), tick "This is a gift" and pay. It appears on their creature within about three hours. The shop then gives you a gift link to send them: it opens a picture of their creature in its new look. The card says "A gift", never who bought it.

How to give a gift Open the shop

Giving

10% of the price of every item sold goes to ocean conservation, paid each month. The shop shows the total given so far once it reaches A$1.

Giveaways

Entry is always free, and the rules are on the giveaway's page. Our giveaways are not run by, or connected with, Instagram or Facebook.

Sponsors

A zone of the ocean may be supported by a partner. A paid one is labelled "Paid for by" and a charity "Partner charity", so it's clear. We only work with brands that are right for young people.

The Ocean of Us is made by one person in Victoria, Australia.
