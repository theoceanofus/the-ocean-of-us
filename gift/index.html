How it works

  1. Find their creature in the shop by its Instagram username, or by the first name and last initial they use on Facebook.
  2. Pick a gift and tick "This is a gift". A pack is three things for the price of two.
  3. Pay through Stripe. It goes on their creature within about three hours.
  4. Tap "Get the gift link" in the shop and send it to them. It opens a picture of their creature in its new look. It never says who paid, so tell them it's from you.

Their creature is always free. A gift is something for a creature that's already in the ocean, never the creature itself.

  • One payment, through Stripe. We never see card details.
  • Nothing is random, and there's no subscription.
  • Refunds for any reason within 14 days.
  • 10% of every sale goes to ocean conservation.

Not in the ocean yet? They can join free: comment an ocean animal on any of our Instagram Reels or Facebook posts.
