About and privacy

The Ocean of Us is a free animated ocean. Comment an animal on one of our Instagram Reels or Facebook posts, or send it to us in a message, and we make you a creature. It isn't run by, or connected with, Instagram, Facebook or Meta.

What we use. Your Instagram username, or on Facebook your name and the number Facebook gives you on our Page, what you wrote in your comment or message, and the creature and looks you asked for. Instagram's and Facebook's tools for account and Page owners give us these.

Why. To make your creature, show it in the ocean and in our videos, and answer your comment or message.

Where it shows. Anyone browsing this site sees your creature and your Instagram username, or for Facebook your first name and last initial, like Sarah K. To hide your name, comment "hide my name" on any of our Reels or posts. It will then say Name hidden, and no part of it appears on the site. Anyone who types your exact name can still find your creature. Comment "show my name" to bring it back. Your name comes off this site within a few hours, and we won't name you in new videos. You can also message us "hide my name" or "show my name" on Instagram (@the_ocean_of_us) or Facebook, or email theoceanofus@gmail.com.

Your comment or message isn't shown on this site. Our Instagram videos may show your Instagram username and your comment, never your messages.

Leaving. Comment "remove me" on any of our Reels or posts, message us on Instagram (@the_ocean_of_us) or Facebook, or email theoceanofus@gmail.com. A parent or guardian can ask too. We take your creature and name off this site and leave you out of new videos. If you want an older video changed, tell us.

What we keep. Once you've left, we delete your name, comments and messages from our working files and keep only a scrambled marker, so your old comments and messages don't add you back by mistake. Your messages also stay in our Instagram or Facebook inbox, as in any chat.

Shop. If you buy an extra, Stripe takes the payment and your card details. We never see them. We keep only the item, the creature it's for, whether it was a gift, the Stripe payment id and the date. Your email stays with Stripe for your receipt and refunds, and we don't use it for anything else. If someone leaves, we take their name off our order records but keep the payment id so a refund can still be made. Stripe keeps its own payment records, including the Instagram username the item was for, or for a creature from Facebook the code in its page's address.

Visitors. This site has no ads or accounts, and things like your Spotted list stay on your phone. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which shows us totals like page views, countries and devices, not who you are. Our host, Cloudflare, logs visitors' IP addresses for security.

Counting taps. We count how many times a few things happen here each day: the shop opening, an item picked, "Try one on", Pay, "Ask someone to buy it", "Copy the link", "Send to a friend", a keepsake picture being opened and a picture to print being made, and, for each zone of the ocean, a visit spending a moment there and a tap on one of its creatures (for our zone partners' monthly report). With each one we note only whether it happened inside the Instagram or Facebook app, whether the shop was opened from an "Ask someone to buy it" link, and how the shop was laid out. We keep just the daily totals, for about 13 months, and never show a total under 5. The counter doesn't know who you are: it uses no cookies and doesn't keep your IP address, anything about your phone or anything else that could say who you are.

Age. The Ocean of Us is for ages 13 and up, the same as Instagram and Facebook. Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before you join or buy anything. If a parent or guardian tells us a creature belongs to a child under 13, we take it and the name off this site and leave them out of new videos.

How it's made. The creatures and this ocean are drawn by computer code, built with help from AI tools. The voice in our Reels is AI-generated. Extinct and mythical creatures are labelled as reconstructions or legends.

Who runs this. The Ocean of Us is made by one person in Victoria, Australia. You can reach us at the email above, or by messaging @the_ocean_of_us on Instagram.
